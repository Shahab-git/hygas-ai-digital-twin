REQUEST FOR INFORMATION — MSW gasification to hydrogen project
To: DOK-ING d.o.o.

Feedstock (5 questions)
   1. How much feedstock (MSW) do you want the system to process per day (tonnes/day)? Is this a fixed rate or does it need to handle seasonal variation?
   2. What is the composition of your feedstock — proximate/ultimate analysis (moisture, ash, volatile matter, fixed carbon, C/H/O/N/S/Cl content) and calorific value (LHV)?
   3. Is the feedstock raw MSW, or has it already been sorted/pre-processed (e.g. RDF, SRF)? Who handles that pre-sorting?
   4. Is feedstock supply guaranteed under contract, or does the plant need to tolerate gaps or substitute feedstocks?
   5. Where does the feedstock come from — a single municipal source, multiple sources, or will you need to purchase/import it?

Hydrogen product (5 questions)
   6. How much hydrogen do you want to produce per day (kg/day) or per year (tonnes/year)?
   7. What purity does the hydrogen need to meet (e.g. 99.9%, 99.999% for fuel cell/mobility grade)?
   8. At what pressure does the hydrogen need to be delivered (storage pressure vs. dispensing pressure)?
   9. What is the intended end use — mobility/refuelling station, industrial feedstock, grid injection/blending, or stationary power via fuel cell?
   10. Do you also want electricity and/or heat as co-products, or is hydrogen the sole target output?

Site and infrastructure (2 questions)
   11. What utilities are already available on site — grid power connection, water supply, existing steam or heat network?
   12. Is there existing waste-handling or industrial infrastructure nearby that this plant needs to interface with?

Regulatory and commercial (4 questions)
   13. What is driving this project — regulatory compliance, decarbonization targets, a new revenue stream, grant funding, or something else?
   14. Does the hydrogen need to qualify as "green" or "low-carbon" under EU RFNBO rules, or is that not a requirement?
   15. What jurisdiction's regulations apply, and do you already hold any permits or environmental approvals?
   16. Is there a target for tonnes of MSW diverted from landfill, independent of the hydrogen output?

Project scope (1 question)
   17. Is there a target budget or capital cost range the design needs to respect?
